\begin{abstract}
    \begin{quotation}

        Conformal prediction provides distribution-free coverage guarantees for uncertainty quantification in deep learning. However, existing multi-label conformal methods either treat labels independently or assume symmetric dependencies, failing to capture the asymmetric relationships between diseases in medical diagnosis.
        We propose Co-occurrence Weighted Conformal Sets (CWCS), a novel conformal prediction method that models asymmetric label dependencies using empirical disease co-occurrence patterns. CWCS produces prediction sets that respect directional clinical relationships, such as infiltration predicting pneumonia more strongly than the reverse.
        We benchmark CWCS against standard conformal prediction, nested sets, and tree-based methods on ChestX-ray14 (14 pathology labels, 112,120 images) using a ResNet-50 classifier. Under severe class imbalance (0.2\%–17.7\% prevalence), all methods achieve valid coverage and similar efficiency. However, CWCS uniquely captures clinically meaningful asymmetric dependencies with measurable directionality, advancing uncertainty quantification for multi-label medical imaging.
        
    \end{quotation}
\end{abstract}
